HPDFViewer — Become a tester

Try new HPDFViewer versions before anyone else and help us improve them. All you need is an Android phone and a Google account.

Join the test

How it works

  1. Open the link above with the Google account you use on your phone and tap Become a tester.
  2. Install or update HPDFViewer from the Play Store: you will get the test version (it can take a few minutes).
  3. If something goes wrong, use Settings → Report a problem in the app, or write to mor.angelo.mor@gmail.com.

You can leave the test at any time from the same link and go back to the public version. Your PDFs and signatures stay on your phone in test versions too (privacy policy).
