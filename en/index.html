From the form received to the form sent, in three steps

HPDFViewer does one thing and does it fast: it lets you fill in and send back a PDF from your phone.

  1. Open

    Tap the PDF in email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Drive or your downloads and choose HPDFViewer. Or scan a sheet of paper.

  2. Fill in and sign

    Tap where you want to write: text, today's date, ticks. You draw your signature once, then add it with one tap.

  3. Share

    One tap creates the filled-in copy and sends it with the app you prefer. The original file stays as it was.

Frequently asked questions

How do you fill in and sign a PDF on Android?

Open the PDF with HPDFViewer (directly from email, WhatsApp, Telegram or Drive too), tap where you want to write and add text, date or ticks. Tap Sign to insert your signature, then Share: the app creates a filled-in copy and sends it with the app you prefer.

Is HPDFViewer free?

Yes. Reading, filling in, signing, scanning and sharing are free, with unobtrusive ads: never full screen, never between pages and never while you fill in or sign. Premium is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, that removes the ads and adds some features.

Are my PDFs uploaded online?

No. HPDFViewer has no accounts and never sends PDFs, scans or signatures over the internet. A document leaves the phone only when you share it with another app. See the privacy policy.

Does a signature added with HPDFViewer have legal value?

It is a simple electronic signature under the eIDAS regulation: the image of your signature drawn on the document. It is not a qualified digital signature, so it does not replace a digital signature where one is expressly required.

Does it work with any PDF, even scanned ones or ones without fillable fields?

Yes. HPDFViewer writes over the page, like a pen on paper, so it also works with scanned forms and with PDFs that have no interactive fields. It does not fill in interactive form fields (AcroForm) as such: you write where you tap.

Is the original file modified?

No, never. When you share or save, HPDFViewer always creates a new copy of the PDF with what you have added.

Can I scan a paper document?

Yes, for free. Scan document photographs the sheets, detects the edges and creates a PDF that you can fill in, sign and share straight away. Pages are processed on the phone and the app does not ask for camera permission. For now scanning is in the beta version: become a tester to use it today.

Which devices does HPDFViewer work on?

On phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later. It is downloaded from Google Play.
